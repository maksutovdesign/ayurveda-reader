spaṣṭārthameva paṭhitamidaṃ kuṣṭhacikitsite salilāḍhake 'ṣṭabhāge śeṣe pūto raso grāhyaḥ te ca kaṣāyāṣṭapale catuṣpalaṃ sarpiṣaśca paktavyam yāvatsyādaṣṭapalaṃ śeṣaṃ peyaṃ tataḥ koṣṇamiti aṣṭabhāgāvaśeṣe tu palānāmaṣṭakadvayam palasya dviguṇe māne kriyamāṇe na yujyate dviguṇe hi pale tat syādekameva palāṣṭakam catuṣpalaṃ ca dātavyaṃ yat proktaṃ saha sarpisā palasya dviguṇe māne tadapyaṣṭapalaṃ bhavet catuḥsuvarṇaṃ hi palaṃ śuṣkāṇāmiṣyate yataḥ dviguṇatvāt dravāṇāṃ hi tat syādaṣṭasuvarṇakam nirūhamātrānirdeśa evaṃ sati ca bādhyate prasṛtārdhaṃ samārabhya dvādaśa prasṛtāḥ param eṣā mātrā dviguṇitā dviguṇe prasṛte bhavet nirūhamātrā śāstroktā dvādaśaprasṛtāstathā catvāriṃśat palāni syuraṣṭābhiradhikāni ca vyādhyādiṣu tu madhyeṣu kvāthasyāñjaliriṣyate atra suśrutanidaśe kvātho 'yaṃ dviguṇo bhavet svarasāt kvathitācchītāt kaṣāyāt ṣaṭpalaṃ pibet yaduktaṃ kharaṇādena taddvādaśapalaṃ bhavet paittike paṭhyate gulme sarpiṣaśca catuṣpalam pibet saṃmūrchitaṃ tena gulmaḥ śāmyati paittikaḥ catuṣpalaṃ ghṛtasyedaṃ mātrāpyaṣṭapalaṃ bhavet svarṇakṣīryādike yoge gomūtrāñjaliriṣyate añjalerdviguṇatvāddhi mātrā sāṣṭapalā bhavet gaulmike trāyamāṇādye ghṛte ca paripaṭhyate
